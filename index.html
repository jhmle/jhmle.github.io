General assistant

Live demo

A hands-free voice assistant. You talk, it listens, looks through your camera and answers out loud. The models run on your device after the first download, with no server and no sign-up.

  • Continuous conversation loop with voice-activity detection that knows when you've finished speaking.
  • Multimodal input: audio and camera frames go to Gemma 4, and replies stream into speech sentence by sentence.
  • Runs on WebGPU with models in Web Workers, so the page stays responsive.

Gemma 4 · Kokoro TTS · transformers.js · WebGPU · Web Audio API · JavaScript
